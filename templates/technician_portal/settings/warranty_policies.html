Warranty Policies

Define warranty terms for different repair types

How it works: When a repair is marked complete, the system automatically assigns a warranty based on the damage type. Lifetime warranties have no expiry; custom-day warranties count from the completion date.

You can have one policy per damage type plus an "All Repairs" fallback default. Mark a policy as default to use it when no damage-type-specific policy exists.

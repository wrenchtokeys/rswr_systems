{% comment %} The "mark the break" control that sits under a photo on the repair detail page. Driven by static/js/photo_crop_detail.js. Params: field (the model field name), photo_url, crop (a RepairPhotoCrop or nothing), prompt (the modal heading, e.g. "Tap the break"). A photo can arrive here without a tap — the prompt was skipped, the customer sent it, or it predates tap-to-crop — so every photo gets the offer, and one that already has a close-up gets to correct it. An unmarked photo also gets a suggested mark when the modal opens (P3); a crop the machine placed and nobody has confirmed says so, because it is a weaker label than a tap and the tech should get the chance to disagree. {% endcomment %}

How your portal works

Everything you can do here, in plain words — two minutes to read.

Approving work

When {{ tenant.name|default:"the shop" }} needs your OK before starting a job, it shows up on your dashboard under Needs approval — and you'll get an email with one-click approve and deny links. Open the job to see the vehicle, the work, and the price, then approve or deny. Nothing is ever done to your vehicles without the price in front of you first.

See what's waiting

Requesting service

Chip in a windshield? Request a repair or a replacement right from the portal — tell us the vehicle, and the shop takes it from there. For replacements, the shop prices the job first and sends it back for your approval, so there are no surprise numbers.

Request a repair Request a replacement

Invoices & paying online

Every invoice lives under Invoices — view it, download the PDF, and if the shop takes card payments, pay it right there with the Pay button. The links in invoice emails work too, no login needed, so your bookkeeper can pay without an account.

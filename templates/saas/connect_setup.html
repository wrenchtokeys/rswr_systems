Frequently Asked Questions

What are the fees?
Stripe charges 2.9% + 30¢ per card transaction. ACH bank transfers are 0.8% (capped at $5). RS Systems does not add additional fees.
When do I get paid?
Stripe typically sends payouts to your bank account within 2 business days of a successful payment.
What if my customer already paid by check?
You can still record manual payments (check, cash, etc.) directly in RS Systems — Stripe is optional and only used when a customer clicks "Pay Online."
Is my customer's card data secure?
Yes. Card details are handled entirely by Stripe — RS Systems never sees or stores payment card information.

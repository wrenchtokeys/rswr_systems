RS Systems Text Message Program

Last updated: October 11, 2026

About this program

RS Systems is job-management and invoicing software for auto glass shops. This program covers text messages RS Systems sends to its own account holders — the shop owners and technicians who sign in to RS Systems — about activity on their own account. Messages come from our dedicated toll-free number and always begin with "RS Systems".

Messages you may receive

  • New repair requests — a customer has asked your shop for work
  • Job assignments — a job has been assigned to you, or reassigned
  • Approvals and denials — a job waiting on approval has been decided
  • Verification codes — a one-time code to confirm the mobile number on your account

Message frequency varies with job activity — typically a few messages per working day. This is not a marketing list; we only text account holders about their own RS Systems account.

How you opt in

Texts are off until you turn them on. Sign in to RS Systems, open Settings → Notifications, check the box labelled "Text me urgent job alerts" and save. The box is never checked for you, and nobody else can check it on your behalf. Your consent is recorded with a timestamp, and no text is sent until you have also verified your mobile number with a one-time code. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of using RS Systems.

Opting out and getting help

  • Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You will receive one final confirmation message.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact RS Systems support using the link below.
  • You can also uncheck the box at Settings → Notifications at any time.

Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Your mobile number and your consent to receive texts are used only to deliver the messages described above. They are never sold, and never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for details.

Contact

Questions about a text message you received? Reach RS Systems support at rssystems.io/contact.

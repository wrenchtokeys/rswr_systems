RS Systems Text Message Program

Last updated: August 9, 2026

About this program

RS Systems is invoicing and job-management software used by auto glass repair shops. When your shop uses RS Systems, it may send you service-related text messages through our platform. Messages come from our dedicated toll-free number and always identify the shop they are sent on behalf of.

Messages you may receive

  • Invoice notifications — a link to view and, where available, pay an invoice for work your shop performed for you
  • Review requests — after a completed job, a one-time invitation to leave the shop a Google review
  • Service updates — occasional updates about work you requested

Message frequency varies with your service activity — typically one or two messages per completed job. This is not a marketing list; we only text customers about their own service with their own shop.

How you opt in

You can opt in yourself: your online invoice page includes a "Get text updates" sign-up where you check a box agreeing to receive service texts at your mobile number. Alternatively, you can provide your mobile number to your auto glass shop and agree to receive service-related texts, and your shop records that consent in RS Systems. Either way, consent is recorded with a timestamp before text messaging is enabled for your account, and consent to receive texts is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

Opting out and getting help

  • Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You will receive one final confirmation message.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact your shop directly.
  • You can also ask your shop to turn off text messaging for your account at any time.

Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Your phone number is used only to deliver the messages described above. It is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for details.

Contact

Questions about text messages you received? Contact your auto glass shop directly (their name appears in every message), or reach RS Systems support at rssystems.io/help/contact.

Platform Fee

Master switch. While this is off, no platform fee is charged on any invoice payment, whatever the rates below say. Turning it on starts charging every shop that has no explicit override — give shops notice first, and consider setting the fee on a single tenant record before flipping this globally.

Percentage taken from each invoice payment (e.g. 2.50 = 2.5%).

Flat amount per transaction, in cents (25 = $0.25). On a $50 chip repair a percentage alone is pennies; percent + fixed is the standard shape. The fee is always clamped so at least $0.50 of the payment remains — Stripe rejects a fee larger than the charge.

A shop with its own override on its tenant record uses its percent and fixed amount as a pair, ignoring both defaults above. Blank (NULL) on a tenant means “use these defaults”; an explicit 0 means “this shop is zero-rated”.

% of subscription payments routed to the competition pool.

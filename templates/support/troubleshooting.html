Why didn't my customer get the invoice email?

Open the invoice — under its status you'll see whether the email was delivered and opened. If it says delivered, ask them to check spam. If it never sent, double-check the email address on their customer page (one typo is the usual culprit), fix it, and send again. Customers can also open the invoice from the link in any earlier email — no login needed.

Why is there no tax on my invoice?

Three possibilities: the shop's tax question hasn't been answered yet in Settings → Pricing & Invoicing, this customer is marked tax-exempt on their customer page, or Charge sales tax was unticked on that job. Fix the one that applies and future invoices come out right.

Why can't my tech see replacements?

Replacement access is a per-tech permission. In Settings → Team, open that technician and turn on replacements — they'll see replacement jobs immediately.

Why is this repair priced lower than my usual rate?

That's progressive pricing doing its job — repeat repairs on the same vehicle step down the price ladder automatically. If you'd rather charge one flat rate every time, that's a single toggle.

Why can't I edit the price on this job?

The job is on an invoice that's already been paid, so its price is locked to keep your books consistent — the note on the job names the invoice. Need to correct it anyway? Adjust the paid invoice from the invoices page rather than the job.

?

My customer paid, but the invoice still shows unpaid

Online card payments mark the invoice paid by themselves. Cash, check, or a bank transfer you took outside RS Systems won't — open the invoice and use Record payment so your books match reality.

Why didn't my customer get a review request?

Review requests go out a little while after a job completes, so give it half an hour first. Then check Settings → Reviews: the program has to be on, and fleet accounts are skipped unless you've enabled Include Fleet Accounts — most shops only ask individual customers for Google reviews.

Why didn't my customer earn points on that job?

Points are awarded the moment a job is marked Completed — jobs still in progress haven't earned yet. If the job is done and there are still no points, the loyalty program is probably switched off on the Loyalty page.

How loyalty works
?

I deleted something by mistake

Nothing is gone forever right away. Deleted jobs, customers, and invoices sit in Recently Deleted for 30 days — open it from Settings, find the item, and restore it with one click.

My customer can't log in to their portal

Most often the invitation was never accepted — resend it from their customer page and have them use the link in the fresh email. And remember: customers don't need a login to see or pay an invoice; the link in the invoice email works on its own.

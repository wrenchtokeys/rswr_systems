Know who owes you, at a glance

The Invoices page leads with "Owed to you" — total outstanding, aged into buckets (current, 1–30, 31–60, 60+ days). Tap a bucket to see exactly which invoices are in it.

Open Invoices
2

Let reminders do the chasing

Turn on overdue reminders in Settings → Pricing & Invoicing and pick when they go out (say, 7 and 30 days past due). Polite, branded, automatic — you stop being the bad guy.

3

Batch invoices for fleets on terms

Fleets that prefer one bill? Batch invoicing bundles a customer's finished jobs into a single invoice on your schedule — weekly, biweekly, or monthly, sent automatically if you want. Set the schedule in Settings → Pricing & Invoicing.

4

One payment, many invoices

When a fleet mails one check covering five invoices, use Receive Payment — enter the amount once and apply it across their open invoices.

Faster still: invoices with a Pay Now button get paid sooner. See Take card payments online.

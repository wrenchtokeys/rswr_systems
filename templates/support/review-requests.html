Paste your Google review link

In Settings → Reviews, switch review requests on and paste your Google review link (Google Business Profile → "Ask for reviews" gives it to you). That's the whole setup.

Open Reviews settings
2

It asks at the right moment

After a job completes, a short branded email goes out — delayed a few hours and only during business hours, so it lands while the good experience is fresh, not at 2am. You can tweak the subject and message.

3

It never pesters

Cooldowns stop repeat asks to the same customer, and fleet accounts are left alone by default (a fleet manager seeing ten review emails a week would not love you) — flip "include fleet accounts" if you want them too.

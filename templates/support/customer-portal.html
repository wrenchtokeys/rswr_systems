Their dashboard, your branding

Customers land on a dashboard with your logo and brand color: recent repairs and replacements with live status, anything waiting on their approval, and their invoice history.

2

They approve priced work

When you price a replacement (or a repair that needs sign-off), it appears in their pending approvals — they review the details and photos, then approve or decline. No phone tag, and there's a written record.

3

They send work to you

Customers can request a repair or a replacement — pick the vehicle, which glass, attach a photo if it helps. You get notified in-app and by email, price it, and it comes back to them for approval.

4

Rewards keep them close

If your loyalty program is on, they see their company's shared points balance, what it's worth, and their referral link. Every completed job adds points automatically.

Getting them in is the easy part — share your join link or invite a contact by email. See Invite your customers.

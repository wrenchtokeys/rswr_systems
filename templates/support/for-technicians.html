Your dashboard is your queue

Log in and you'll see the jobs assigned to you. The bell shows new assignments. Jobs in the top nav lists everything you can work on.

2

Log work from the truck

The job form works great on your phone: the + button starts a job, photo fields open your camera directly, and the form autosaves as you type — losing signal in a parking garage won't lose your work.

3

Photos tell the story

Before and after shots protect you and the shop. Mark the damage location by tapping the windshield diagram. Forgot a photo? Add it later from the job's page.

4

Resin suggestions, if your shop uses them

Type the windshield temperature and (when your shop has resin rules on) a colored badge suggests the viscosity — like "🟢 Medium — ideal conditions." Follow it or use your judgment.

Multiple chips on one windshield? Use multi-break entry — shared info once, one card per break, priced automatically.

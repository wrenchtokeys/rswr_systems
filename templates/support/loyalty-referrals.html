1

Turn it on, set the earn rate

Open Loyalty in the top nav. Switch the program on, choose how many points a dollar of completed work earns, and create your reward options ("$25 off", "free chip repair" — whatever fits your shop).

Open Loyalty
2

Points belong to the company

Balances are per customer company, not per person — the whole fleet office sees one number, and customers earn even before anyone has a portal login. Every completed job adds points automatically.

3

Redeem at the counter

When a customer has enough points, the job form prompts you; you (or a manager) can also apply a reward from the customer's page. Changed your mind? Redemptions can be cancelled and refunded from Loyalty.

4

Referrals pay both sides

Every customer gets a referral link. When a business they refer signs up and completes its first job, both companies earn bonus points — automatically, no tracking spreadsheet.

Quiet marketing: flip on "show balance in emails" and invoices mention the customer's points — a little reminder that loyalty pays, on every bill.

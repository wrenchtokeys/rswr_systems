1

When to use it

One windshield, more than one break — three chips on unit 1001, a crack plus a star break, damage found walking a lot. (Different vehicles? Log separate jobs instead.)

Open multi-break entry
2

Fill in the shared info once

Customer, unit number, date — entered once for the whole session. A pricing preview appears as soon as customer and unit are set, so you can quote the total before you start.

3

Add each break

Tap Add Break for every damage point: damage type, optional photos (your phone camera works right in the form), temperature and resin notes if you track them. Each break shows as a card you can review before saving.

4

Pricing steps down automatically

With progressive pricing on, break #1 bills at your first-repair price, #2 at your second, and so on — the same discount your fleet customers expect, applied without any math. All breaks save together and invoice as one line-itemed job.

Photos can wait. Missing before/after photos are flagged on each break's card — you can add them later from the job's detail page.

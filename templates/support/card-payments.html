1

Connect your bank once

Go to Settings → Card Payments and click Set up payments. You'll answer a few identity questions from Stripe (the payments company behind millions of businesses) and link your bank account. Takes about five minutes.

Open Card Payments settings
2

Every invoice grows a Pay Now button

Once connected, invoice emails and the online invoice page show Pay Now. Customers pay by card without creating an account or calling you back.

3

Money goes straight to you

Payments land in your Stripe account and pay out to your bank on Stripe's normal schedule — RS Systems never holds your money. Paid invoices are marked automatically, so your "Owed to you" number stays honest.

Prefer cash and checks? Card payments are optional. You can always record manual payments on any invoice — see Send an invoice.

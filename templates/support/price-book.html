1

It learns from work you finish

Every replacement you complete with a year, make and model teaches the price book what you charge for that glass on that vehicle. There is nothing to fill in up front — the first job starts the book, and it grows as you work. Find it under Settings → Pricing & Invoicing → Open price book.

Open the price book {% icon 'arrow-right' class="text-xs ml-1.5" %}
2

It suggests; it never overrides you

Start a replacement on a vehicle you've done before and an empty price box fills in with a note saying where the number came from, and an Undo. If you've already typed a price, the box is left alone and the note just offers Use $X beside it. Whatever ends up in the box is what's charged.

3

Pin a price to make it stick

A learned price moves with your latest completed job. If you want a number to stay put no matter what, Add a price or Edit one in the book — a price you set by hand is pinned and no job will overwrite it. Leave the year blank to cover every year of that model.

4

Rebuild from history any time

Rebuild from history re-reads every completed replacement and refreshes the learned prices. Pinned prices are kept. Prices in the book are before any fleet discount — the discount still comes off on the job.

Open the job form

From your dashboard, hit New Repair or New Replacement (on your phone, use the + button at the bottom). One form handles both kinds of work.

Open the job form
2

Say who it's for

Pick a fleet account, or switch to Individual for a walk-in. New person? Type their name right there — no separate "create customer" detour. If they look like someone you've served before, we'll suggest the match so you don't end up with duplicates.

3

Let pricing do its thing

Leave the price blank on a repair and your price book fills it in automatically. Type a number any time to override it. Sales tax (if you charge it) is calculated for you.

4

Save — or save and get paid

Save Job logs the work. Save & Send Invoice does the whole thing: completes the job, creates the invoice, and emails it with a pay-online link. Photos, damage location, and notes live under More details if you want them.

Several chips on one windshield? Use multi-break entry instead — each break is priced progressively and they invoice together.

1

Invite by email

In Settings → My Team, click Invite Member — name, email, role. They get a link (good for 7 days) to set a password and they're in. If their email bounces, copy the invite link and text it to them instead.

Open My Team
2

Pick the right role

Technician — does the work: their own queue, logging jobs, photos. Manager — runs the day-to-day: everything you see except billing and plan changes, and they can invite techs. Owner — that's you: everything, including the subscription.

3

Two switches per tech

Does repairs and does replacements control what work each tech can take. A repair-only tech never sees replacement forms, and auto-assignment only routes them jobs they can do.

4

Choose how jobs get assigned

In Settings → My Shop, pick an assignment style: manual, primary-tech-first (default — customers can have a go-to tech), balanced (fewest active jobs wins), or round-robin. Techs get an in-app notification when work lands on them.

Working solo? You're already set up as a technician — nothing to configure. Add people when you're ready.

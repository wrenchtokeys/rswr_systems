My Shop

Your business name, phone, email, address, logo, and brand color — this is what customers see on invoices, emails, and their portal. It's also where job assignment lives: who gets new work automatically (primary tech first, balanced, round-robin, or manual).

My Team

Invite technicians and managers by email. Each tech has two switches — does repairs and does replacements — that control which jobs they can be assigned. Managers can run the day-to-day but can't touch billing.

Pricing & Invoicing

Set your repair prices — one flat price, or progressive pricing that steps down for multiple repairs on the same unit ($50 → $40 → $35…). Answer the sales-tax question once and every job calculates it for you. Invoice numbering, payment terms, overdue reminders, and batch invoicing for fleets all live here too.

Card Payments

Connect a Stripe account and every invoice email gets a Pay Now button. Money goes straight to your bank — RS Systems never holds your funds.

Warranty & Reviews

Warranty sets the promise printed on invoices for repairs and replacements. Reviews sends a polite "how'd we do?" email with your Google review link after completed jobs — set it and forget it.

Loyalty

The Loyalty page (top nav) runs your points program: earning rate, reward options, and referral bonuses. Points belong to the customer's company, so everyone there sees one shared balance.

Defaults are safe. Anything marked "Using standard defaults" on the checklist works out of the box — you're never blocked on a setting to start logging jobs.

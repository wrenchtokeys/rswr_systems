Share your shop's join link

In Settings you'll find your shop's personal join link (it looks like rssystems.io/join/your-shop/). Text it, email it, put it on your card — customers sign themselves up and land in your portal, branded with your logo and color.

2

Or invite a specific contact

From a customer's page you can invite their billing or fleet contact by email. They get a personal link, set a password, and they're in — already connected to the right company.

3

What customers can do in the portal

Track every repair and replacement with live status, approve work you've priced, request new work (with photos), see invoices, and watch their rewards balance grow. Primary contacts get email updates as jobs move.

Referrals are built in. Every portal customer gets a referral code — when a business they refer completes its first job, both sides earn reward points automatically.

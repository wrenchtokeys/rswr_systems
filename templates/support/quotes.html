1

Price the work first

Open Quotes in the top nav and hit New Quote. Pick the customer and vehicle, add a line per piece of work, and set the price. Nothing is scheduled and nothing is charged — a quote is a number the customer can say yes to.

Open Quotes {% icon 'arrow-right' class="text-xs ml-1.5" %}
2

Send it — by email or by link

Send quote emails it. Prefer texting? The quote page also gives you a link you can copy — anyone with it can view and accept, no login. Customers with a portal account see the same quote under Quotes when they sign in.

3

They accept, the jobs appear

When the customer taps Accept, RS Systems creates the jobs for you — already approved, at the quoted price, locked so nobody re-prices them by accident. They land in the queue like any other work. Said yes on the phone instead? Use Mark accepted & create jobs on the quote and it does the same thing.

4

Quotes expire; revise, don't edit

A quote stays open for a set number of days (30 to start — change it in Settings → Pricing & Invoicing) because glass prices move. Once sent, a quote is never edited: Revise makes a fresh draft that replaces it, so the customer always sees one current number and you keep the history. Only a draft that was never sent can be deleted.

{% icon 'lightbulb' class="text-amber-500 mt-0.5" %}

Fleet on a discount? Type the price the customer will actually pay. Their account discount is already in the number on the quote, and the jobs come out at exactly that.

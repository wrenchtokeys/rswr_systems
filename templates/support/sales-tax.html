Answer the question once

Some shops charge sales tax, some don't — both are fine. Until you answer, a banner reminds you and invoices go out with no tax. Set it in Settings → Pricing & Invoicing: flip tax on and enter your rate, or tell us you don't charge it and the banner goes away.

Open tax settings
2

Then it's automatic

With tax on, every job calculates it for you and invoices show the line item. No per-job math, ever.

3

Exceptions are one checkbox

Tax-exempt customer (government fleet, reseller)? Mark them exempt on their customer page and their jobs skip tax automatically. One-off situation? Untick Charge sales tax right on the job form.

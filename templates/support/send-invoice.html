1

The fastest way: from the job itself

On the job form, Save & Send Invoice completes the job, builds the invoice, and emails it in one tap. Already-saved job? Open it and use Complete & Invoice — same result.

2

What your customer gets

A clean, branded email with a link to view the invoice online — job details, photos, and a Pay Now button if you've connected card payments (Settings → Card Payments). No login needed on their end.

3

Track it from Invoices

The Invoices page shows every invoice with its status — sent, viewed, paid, overdue. The "Owed to you" card up top totals what's outstanding, and overdue reminders can go out automatically (Settings → Pricing & Invoicing).

Open Invoices
4

Recording payments

Paid by card online? It's marked automatically. Cash or check? Use Record Payment on the invoice, or the Receive Payment screen to apply one payment across several invoices.

Fleet customers on terms? Batch invoicing (Settings → Pricing & Invoicing) can bundle a fleet's jobs into one invoice on a schedule instead of emailing per job.
